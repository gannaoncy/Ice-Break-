## 📌 Project Overview
**ice break** is a graduation project for the **Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI)** under the *Digital Marketing Specialist* track. This project establishes a comprehensive digital marketing and business expansion strategy for a premium drive-thru iced coffee brand operating in New Cairo. The business addresses the growing demand for convenient, high-quality, plant-based coffee tailored for university students and busy commuters. By combining hyper-local convenience, 100% natural ingredients, targeted pop-culture activations (such as Point 90 Cinema partnerships and Sahel summer activations), and structured strategic planning (BMC, SWOT, PESTEL, and Buyer Personas), the project models a scalable 3-month rollout across 5 key sectors in New Cairo.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Conduct in-depth market research, PESTEL analysis, and SWOT analysis to establish a strong positioning framework for **ice break** in New Cairo.  
- Formulate a detailed Business Model Canvas (BMC) and create data-driven Buyer Personas representing primary target segments (students and working professionals).  
- Design high-impact seasonal and pop-culture marketing campaigns (Point 90 Cinema Spider-Man Activation, Mawlid Pairing Special, and Sahel Summer Campaign).  
- Establish a value-based pricing tier and digital promotional strategy targeting 5 sector expansions within 3 months.  

---

## 📅 Project Plan (5 Weeks)

### 🗓️ Week 1 — Foundation, Research & Market Analysis
- Defined brand vision, mission, SMART goals, and Unique Selling Proposition (USP).  
- Executed PESTEL analysis focusing on local regulations, economic factors, and health/plant-based consumer trends in New Cairo.  
- Conducted SWOT analysis and competitive landscape mapping against local and international coffee chains.  

### 🗓️ Week 2 — Business Modeling & Buyer Persona Development
- Developed comprehensive Business Model Canvas (BMC) covering all 9 strategic building blocks.  
- Formulated detailed Buyer Personas for key segments: "The Health-Conscious Student" and "The Fast-Paced Professional".  
- Structured value-based pricing tiers for core coffee lines versus specialty plant-based signature drinks.  

### 🗓️ Week 3 — Campaign Ideation & Strategic Activation Planning
- Designed the "Break The Verse" Spider-Man Cinema Activation strategy partnering with Point 90 Cinema.  
- Created the "Sweeten & Elevate" Mawlid pairing offer mechanics and bounce-back coupon retention program.  
- Planned the dual-shift "Coast Control" Sahel summer campaign for beach and night-event coverage.  

### 🗓️ Week 4 — Visual Content Direction & Collateral Design
- Created visual prompts, drink design concepts, and packaging specifications for limited-edition signatures.  
- Produced high-fidelity campaign visual assets, drink mockups, and drive-thru logo placement guidelines.  
- Developed social media copy, hashtags, and engagement hooks for TikTok and Instagram reels.  

### 🗓️ Week 5 — Final Portfolio Assembly & DEPI Presentation
- Integrated all market analysis, campaign frameworks, visual mockups, and execution plans into the final project documentation.  
- Finalized GitHub documentation (`README.md`), project presentation slides, and pitch deck.  
- Conducted final review and delivered graduation presentation for DEPI evaluation.
